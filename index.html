Understanding the metrics

Precision

Of all transactions predicted as fraud, how many were actually fraud.

Recall

Of all actual fraud transactions, how many the model detected.

F1 score

A balance between precision and recall.

PR-AUC

Measures precision-recall performance, useful for imbalanced datasets.
